# An image's facts are a union checked at the consumer

Lands: a third world — beyond an export and a daemon reference —
reaches plugin.Image or runner.Spec

plugin.Image holds an exported rootfs or a reference the daemon runs
with its pull flag, and the platform admitted for either; that
exactly one of the rootfs and the reference is set is a rule the
runner's spec check enforces after the fact, and the producers keep
by construction. A sum shape — an export with its platform, or a
daemon image with its reference, pull flag and platform — would make
the rule unrepresentable where the facts are made, the encoding
ladder's preference over a check. With two worlds the union is one
field per fact and the check one rule, pinned by the runner suite;
a third world is the point at which the arms outgrow that rule and
the sum becomes the shape: the acquisition result and the runner's
spec taking it alike, the verb's projection typed per arm.
